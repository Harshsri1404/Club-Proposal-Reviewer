CLUB COUNCIL - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS FOR REVIEWERS
Autumn Term 2026 (fictional document for a learning project)

Q1. How are the 14 days in R-01 counted?
Count calendar days from the submission date to the event date. A proposal submitted on 1 October for an event on 15 October has exactly 14 days and is acceptable.

Q2. How are volunteers counted under R-15?
Divide the expected attendance by 25 and round up. For 60 attendees the club needs 3 volunteers. For 20 attendees the answer is 1, but the minimum of 2 applies.

Q3. How is the capacity limit in R-13 worked out?
Take 80 percent of the venue capacity. The Library Hall holds 60, so at most 48 attendees are allowed. The Seminar Hall holds 120, so at most 96 are allowed.

Q4. A proposal does not mention sponsors, guests, fees or food. Is that a problem?
No. Assume there are none. Only rules that require a statement (R-02, R-06, R-12, R-14, R-22) can be broken by silence.

Q5. A proposal has no faculty coordinator name, or says it will be decided later.
This is a missing detail under R-02.

Q6. How is the miscellaneous limit in R-08 checked?
Divide the miscellaneous line by the total budget. Exactly 10 percent is allowed. Above 10 percent is a Minor issue.

Q7. A proposal breaks several rules. What do we do?
List every broken rule. The verdict follows the most severe one. Do not report the same problem under two different rules unless both rules really apply.

Q8. When are posters a problem under R-21?
Only when the proposal says the posters lack the required wording, or says they were put up before approval.

Q9. What counts as a hazardous activity under R-16?
Bonfires and other open flames, climbing or rope courses higher than 2 metres, any activity with motor vehicles, and any activity in or on water.

Q10. Does an event that starts before an exam week and ends inside it break R-03?
Yes. R-03 applies if any part of the event falls on an exam-week day.

Q11. Should the reviewer judge whether the event is a good idea?
No. The Council decides only using the rulebook. Do not add personal opinions about the topic, prizes or publicity.
